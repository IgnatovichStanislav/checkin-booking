# Booking data and decisions

## Logical records

| Record and key | Scope, values and lifetime |
| --- | --- |
| Hotel integration | Authenticated providerId, permitted propertyIds, permissions, hotel timeZoneId, registration policy and pinned destination choices. Key material is a runtime file. Salou resolves one hotel before mapping. |
| Source booking and BookingDto | Unique tuple (providerId, propertyId, srcBookingId) maps to one internal UUIDv7 bookingId. BookingDto carries that tuple, createdAt, updatedAt, arrivalDate, departureDate, reservationStatus, cancelledAt and the full typed guest list. Store timestamps at the supported tick precision. |
| Guest participation | Unique (bookingId, srcGuestId) maps to guestId. Rows survive omission. Membership episodes preserve restoration and previous attendance. |
| Restricted accepted snapshot | Immutable snapshotId identifies one accepted change. Isolate synthetic names, dates of birth, passport and restricted conflict inputs. Link restricted versions by scoped snapshot and guest IDs. All versions and conflicts share the applicable erasure policy. |
| Outbox and deduplication | One stable eventId per accepted change refers to snapshotId and bookingId. Persist the immutable event-to-snapshot binding. Consumer effect key includes event and effect kind, with a unique guard. Late delivery must load that scoped version, or record a safe expired/obsolete outcome after erasure. |
| Attendance | Append-only attendanceEventId, bookingId, guestId, episode, authenticated actor, status, occurredAt and receivedAt. A correction additionally references originalEventId and reasonCode. Keep the original. |
| Registration operation | Stable operationId and registrationRequestId, scoped guest, kind, pinned destination, snapshot reference, state, reason, prior operation, attempted content reference and attempt history. Reuse the request ID on unchanged retries. Distinguish new changed-content submissions. |
| Notification and delivery | Stable notificationId for one public registration change. Unique (subscriptionId, notificationId) delivery with separate attempts. Own each subscription and key reference by hotel. Limit public delivery history to safe codes and timestamps. |

## Decision order

1. Resolve ownership and action permission first. Return 401 for absent/invalid credentials, 403 for insufficient action permission and 404 for an inaccessible or absent scoped resource, including a guest outside its parent. Reject payload scope selectors. Validate bounded bytes, JSON shape, explicit required/null fields, Unicode scalar lengths, valid ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 membership, dates and cross-field constraints. Preserve text exactly and reject empty required text. Compare dates of birth with today's date in the hotel zone. An expired passport remains valid intake.
2. POST has its own semantics. An absent source key creates. Identical business data including updatedAt returns existing mappings. Every different existing snapshot returns 409 with the owned update URL, including an older or newer one. For equal-time differences persist a conflict and queue reconciliation. PUT resolves the internal ID, rejects a changed srcBookingId or createdAt, then applies newer, ignores older, deduplicates equal-identical and records equal-different as a conflict. Return PUT 200 for apply, stale or duplicate with accepted mappings. A conflict preserves accepted state.
3. Compare every typed source field, including restricted fields and null passport. Compare instants and dates by value and text ordinally. Match guests by distinct srcGuestId and compare sets plus guest values structurally. Objects, lists and generated record equality alone do not establish structural equality. Ignore diagnostics, eventId and generated IDs. No hash-only conflict decision.
4. In the future host use a unique source tuple and transaction guard for concurrent inserts, then lock the scoped booking and compare against the committed winner on retry. In one transaction write booking, restricted snapshot and outbox. A conflict records protected evidence and reconciliation intent before 409. Publisher marks publication only after Kafka confirmation. Consumer advances offsets contiguously after its transaction or durable dead-letter handoff. Replays reference the accepted change even if a newer change exists.
5. A cancelled booking cancels all current members. On confirmed snapshots omit → cancel, reappear → reuse guestId, begin a fresh expected attendance episode and reassess registration. Preserve prior episodes. Deduplicate attendance against the full approved tuple across history before checking chronology. New reports require active membership and a permitted transition in the G1 table. Equal-time contradictions, older unseen reports and ordinary reversals return 409. A correction posts to the scoped event's corrections collection with replacement status, UTC occurredAt and a controlled non-identifying reasonCode. Include target and reason in retry equivalence. Append with actor and receipt time. Recompute the episode by replacing the targeted observation at its original position, retaining later valid observations. Reject incompatible later history with 409 for reconciliation. Historical-episode corrections never change a restored episode.
6. Registration uses independent pending/confirmed/failed states and reasons. Current operation starts pending. Missing or expired passport (expiresOn before hotel-local today) yields actionRequired. Known temporary failure or uncertain outcome yields processing. Reconcile uncertainty before any resend. Five submissions total; delay 5s/30s/2m/10m from the preceding definitive transient failure. A reconciliation lookup is not a submission. Record attempts before network calls and treat ambiguous completion as uncertain. Exhaustion or unsupported action yields failed. Authorized manual retry of failed work reuses identity/destination and opens an audited fresh attempt series. Corrections to rejected content require distinct content lineage. Before sending use the latest relevant accepted input. After possible submission reconcile the pinned operation first. After confirmation create a distinct required amendment/cancellation at the original destination. Cancellation stops unsent work. Ignore obsolete results for other operations. Never change attendance on any registration transition.
7. For GET, return opaque mappings with safe states. Outstanding cancellation entries remain visible independently of active summary. Apply the four-status priority and the no-operation rules in research.md. Persist each public registration change with its notification atomically. Restrict operation/delivery management, callback signing and destination details to their own permissions.

## Retention clock

For a confirmed booking the deadline is the first valid instant of hotel-local midnight at departureDate + 30 calendar days. If midnight falls in a gap, use the first valid instant on that date; at an overlap use the earlier UTC instant. For a cancelled booking use source cancelledAt + 30 × 24 hours, exactly. Omitted guests on a confirmed booking retain the checkout-based deadline. Use the reservation's accepted local checkout or source cancellation, independent of operation state, arrival time and retries. At now >= deadline block identity reads and remove restricted material, including old versions and conflicts. Persist only minimal identity-free history. Do not include identity in errors or store unkeyed identity hashes.

### C01 · request at or after the retention boundary (G2 approved)

Scope, required input, guest-parent and immutable-field validation still apply where decidable from safe metadata. Evaluate each deadline at the transaction decision instant. The exact deadline counts as expired. First apply the existing accepted state's erasure clock independently of the submitted snapshot. A rejected request never postpones that clock or stores its guest fields. Retain scoped mappings, source times and the erasure marker. No identity-derived content verifier survives erasure.

| Authorized valid input | Approved response | Durable effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First POST, incoming deadline reached | 410 identity_erased | No booking, identity or booking outbox write |
| Existing POST with a different updatedAt, including an erased booking | 409 use_put with the owned updatePath | No booking or outbox effect. The timestamps establish a difference. |
| Existing POST or PUT at the accepted updatedAt after erasure | 410 identity_erased after recording comparisonUnavailable | Atomically open or join an unresolved reconciliation intent for this scoped booking and instant. No booking change, identity retention or booking outbox write. Failed handoff cannot return 410. |
| Stale PUT, including after erasure | 200 with current mappings | No booking or outbox effect |
| Newer PUT with incoming deadline reached, including a live booking whose proposed deadline moves into the past | 410 identity_erased | Reject incoming changes. Preserve the prior booking subject to its own erasure deadline. No guest identity or booking outbox write for the request. |
| Newer PUT with a future deadline, including legitimate reactivation after erasure | 200 after acceptance | Apply the new snapshot under its own deadline. Erased versions remain unavailable. |
| Live first import or other live comparison | G1 POST/PUT decision table | Accepted changes and equal-time conflicts follow the approved transaction rules. |

**Before C01.** G1 distinguishes equal-time duplicates and collisions by full content and requires durable recording and authoritative reconciliation for conflicts. After erasure an identical retry and an equal-time collision can be indistinguishable. Treat every such indeterminate input as a potential conflict. Retain only the scope, accepted instant, safe reason and reconciliation state. Coalesce only an actively unresolved intent of the same scope and instant. A closed intent cannot suppress a later arrival. Surface the case and consult the authoritative source without rehydrating expired data. Leave the case unresolved when the source cannot establish the outcome. Replay, retry and operational delays cannot restore erased identity.

C01 changes Salou responses after expiry. Future provider contracts must specify their equivalent behavior. This interpretation preserves constitution III's conflict handling without a tie-break or waiver. The Owner approved C01 at G2 on 2026-11-24.

### C02 · stopping visible unsent work (G2 approved)

When cancellation stops an unsent operation that was publicly visible, retire it as current and atomically enqueue a registration-change notification. GET has no applicable operation and returns null for operationId, registrationStatus and reasonCode. The callback identifies the historical stopped operationId, sets registrationStatus to null and reasonCode to registration_stopped. Subscribers include null in eventStates to receive this transition. An initially disabled hotel with no operation has no transition to publish. Disabling a hotel later affects new work only. Current pinned or confirmed operations retain their status, visibility, destination and retry policy. Remove a completed cancellation from the outstanding list and retain its history internally.

G1 reserves planning detail for stopped/disabled work. The C02 wire contract affects callback consumers, filters and GET projections. The Owner approved C02 at G2 on 2026-11-24. The four named public statuses are unchanged.
